Known from the product page only: K230 SoC with two 64-bit RISC-V cores
(CPU1 at 1.6 GHz, CPU0 at 800 MHz) plus KPU, GPU and VPU; a 4.1" 568x1232
AMOLED (RM69A10 driver IC) with touch; LoRa; a camera; HDMI. RAM, storage,
radios and the panel interface are not on the product page.

## Hardware checklists

Human-run; images and scripts are prepared before each step.

### M30: bring-up gate

- [ ] Our image boots on the big core to the shim's banner and `iex(1)>` on the
  serial console.
- [ ] The little core runs a trivial image we loaded.

### M31: inter-core link

- [ ] Ping across the link.
- [ ] Distribution comes up over it.
- [ ] Latency and throughput recorded.

### M32: AtomVM on the little core

- [ ] The little core boots AtomVM.
- [ ] It joins Elixos.
- [ ] It survives a BEAM crash on the big core.

### M33: the deck

- [ ] Boots to IEx on the AMOLED.
- [ ] Touch and the on-screen keyboard work.
- [ ] `Node.list()` shows the coprocessor.
- [ ] A LoRa message arrives as a message.
- [ ] Killing the BEAM keeps the panel alive and the session comes back.
- [ ] Save, reboot, still there.
- [ ] Poweroff powers off.
